HOW TO READ A STAR

Size is skill. Brightness is interest.

Every star tells you two things. How big it is shows how strong your kid got at the skill. How bright it is shows how much it lit them up. The two don't always match, and that's the point.

Big and bright

Strong and loves it. A true strength. Build on it.

Small and bright

New at it, but lit up. A spark worth feeding.

Big and dim

Good at it, not excited by it. A tool, not a calling.

Small and dim

Tried it. Now they know. That's worth knowing too.

HOW A SESSION RUNS · 90 MINUTES · L.I.F.T.

See something amazing. Then do it yourself.

Every session sparks two things at once: a real-world career kids may never have heard of, and a durable skill they'll use no matter what they become.

  1. 0 - 15 MIN

    Launch

    A fast, hands-on challenge that opens the door to a new world.

  2. 15 - 30 MIN

    Inspire

    The expert shows something jaw-dropping from their real work.

  3. 30 - 75 MIN

    Figure It Out

    Kids take on a kid-sized version of the real thing. They try, miss, adjust and try again, with the expert beside them.

  4. 75 - 90 MIN

    Teach Back

    Kids teach it back to prove they own it, earn their star, and rate how much it lit them up.

INSIDE A SESSION

Four afternoons, up close.

The Chef

SAMPLE · WEEK 01
LAUNCH
Blind taste test. Can you name all five tastes, including umami?
INSPIRE
The chef turns fruit juice into tiny pearls that pop like caviar, using a real technique from modern restaurant kitchens.
FIGURE IT OUT
Kids make their own juice pearls, then plan and plate a dessert around them.
TEACH BACK
Each kid serves their plate to a parent judge and explains the recipe step by step.
LIFE SKILLFollowing a recipe and planning a meal
DURABLE SKILLCreativity
CAREER SPARKSChef, food scientist, restaurant owner

The Carpenter

SAMPLE · WEEK 02
LAUNCH
Build the tallest tower you can from 20 sheets of paper in 5 minutes.
INSPIRE
A carpenter assembles a sturdy stool with no nails or screws, using wooden joints that lock together like puzzle pieces.
FIGURE IT OUT
Kids measure, cut with kid-safe hand tools, sand and assemble their own small box.
TEACH BACK
Stand test: does it hold a stack of books? Each kid explains one measurement they got right.
LIFE SKILLMeasuring, planning and building
DURABLE SKILLFortitude
CAREER SPARKSCarpenter, engineer, architect

The Founder

SAMPLE · WEEK 03
LAUNCH
Sell me this pencil. Thirty seconds, go.
INSPIRE
A founder walks kids through a real product, from the first sketch on a napkin to the box on the shelf.
FIGURE IT OUT
Kids invent a product, figure out what it costs to make, and set a price.
TEACH BACK
A 60-second pitch to a panel of parents, who invest with play money.
LIFE SKILLPitching an idea and pricing it
DURABLE SKILLCommunication
CAREER SPARKSEntrepreneur, marketer, product designer

The Surgeon

SAMPLE · WEEK 06
LAUNCH
Steady hands: move ten dried beans from one bowl to another with chopsticks in 60 seconds.
INSPIRE
A surgeon shows how doctors practice stitches on a banana peel before they ever touch a patient, then ties a surgeon's knot one-handed.
FIGURE IT OUT
Kids scrub in with a real surgical handwash, stitch a banana peel with blunt needles, then clean and cover a practice cut.
TEACH BACK
Each kid teaches a parent the surgical handwash and how to clean and cover a cut.
LIFE SKILLCaring for a cut: clean, cover, check
DURABLE SKILLMindfulness
CAREER SPARKSSurgeon, nurse, physician assistant, veterinarian

WHY WE CALL IT A LAUNCH

Every ending is a beginning.

Every session opens with a Launch, because every new world starts with a first step. And every time a kid proves a skill, they're ready for the next one.

We're raising serial launchers: kids who know how to finish strong, when to close a chapter, and how to open the next one with confidence. The biggest Launch of all, the Constellation Launch, comes at the end.

THE CONSTELLATION REPORT

See what they're good at, and what lit them up.

At the end, every family gets a Constellation Report: a constellation of the durable skills they built, one star per expert session, each sized by skill and lit by interest. Plus one North Star Goal for what's next. Halfway through, a check-in shows you how it's going.
