How It Works

Two sides. One bar.

For Programs

Book a Catalyst

  1. Tell us the need. Your kids, their ages, the day and the theme.
  2. Get matched. We send Catalysts who already cleared the bar, with their ratings.
  3. They run a L.I.F.T. session. Launch, Inspire, Figure It Out, Teach Back.
  4. See the growth. Every kid's star goes into their Constellation Report, and you rate the session.
Tell Us What You Need
For Catalysts

Become a Catalyst

  1. Apply. Tell us what you do and the real tools you'd bring.
  2. Get vetted. A background check, references and a real interview.
  3. Get trained. We help you find the moments in your work that light a kid up, and shape them into a L.I.F.T. session.
  4. Get observed, then placed. We watch you teach before your first booking, and every session after is rated.
Apply to Be a Catalyst
Person by Person

We vet people, not employers.

Working at a great program doesn't make someone a Catalyst, and neither does having a great job. Kids don't separate the adult from the career, so one bad hour can close a door for good. Every Catalyst earns their place on their own.

NOT ENOUGH ON ITS OWN

Works for a well-known program, has an impressive title, or is a parent at the school.

WHAT MAKES A CATALYST

An interesting job, the real tools to show it, great with kids, able to teach, and able to find the moments in their work that light a kid up.

RelationshipsKids trust them fast. They learn names and pull in the kid hanging back.
EngagementKids' hands are busy, not just their eyes, and they ask for another round.
RigorKids learn the real skill at their size, and every kid can teach one thing back.

Four stars or better on all three, or no placement.
